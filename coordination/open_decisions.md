  - **H-DEC-04B — Send Receipt/Attempt single-entry boundary (API+DB):** **TEST_BASELINE_APPROVED:** one accepted Send/Receipt may have **zero or one** valid, linked Attempt in this first package. Existing prior multi-Attempt records are **preserved unchanged** and flagged as anomalies (fail closed on further scheduling); future multiple Attempts/engine re-execution are explicitly out of scope. **NEEDS_API_DB_REVIEW:** physical FK/UNIQUE, concurrent T2 safety, historical anomaly and versioned retry semantics.

A client-issued Poll sequence does **not** prove server commit order.
  - **H-DEC-04D — Create immutable creation snapshot and current authorization (API+DB):** **TEST_BASELINE_APPROVED:** replay is original creation snapshot, *not* mutable current Conversation data; current owner, access and nondeleted state must be revalidated before release. Retention remains open.
